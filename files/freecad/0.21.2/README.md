# FreeCAD 0.21.2 fixtures, recipes v1.30

Small, known FreeCAD files made from the CAD Fixture Recipes booklet (v1.30) in FreeCAD 0.21.2: the P, S, F, and N series (FreeCAD 0.21 has no built-in assemblies). Parts are PartDesign bodies with fully constrained sketches; sheet metal uses the SheetMetal add-on 0.8.26; assemblies use the built-in Assembly workbench and link the part files beside them; the N series covers what only FreeCAD documents hold (sketch curves, expressions and spreadsheets, Part containers, groups, and links, PartDesign transforms, hidden binders, and every Sketcher constraint). Every file was reopened and checked against the booklet's expected values. manifest.json lists each file's expected and measured values, its features, sketches, and joints (for N files also the expected solid and visible-body counts and the measured area and bodies), and the recipes left out, with a reason.
